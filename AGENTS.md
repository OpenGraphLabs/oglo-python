## GitHub issue planning

- Write issue bodies and completion comments in English, using `.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/issue.md` and `.github/ISSUE_COMPLETION_TEMPLATE.md`.
- When one goal contains multiple independently implementable and verifiable outcomes, create or reuse sub-issues and confirm their native GitHub parent-child relationships. Links in the body alone do not establish that relationship. Do not split a small single task unnecessarily.
- Keep the overall goal and acceptance criteria in the parent. Give each sub-issue its own scope and acceptance criteria.
- Before work begins, assign every issue and sub-issue to `kkdeok` and verify the assignment. Use exactly one of `feat`, `fix`, `chore`, `docs`, `refactor`, or `test` as its label, with the same label in the title prefix.
- Close a parent issue only after its sub-issues are closed and its overall acceptance criteria have been verified. Closed sub-issues alone do not establish completion.
