STEP 2 OF 6 · CALIBRATION

Calibrate both gloves

A sweep measures the pressure caused by wearing and bending each glove. Choose a fresh sweep when the fit has changed, or explicitly reuse a saved baseline that still matches the fit.

For a fresh sweep
  1. Wear both gloves and hold your hands clear of objects.
  2. Start the sweep, then repeatedly open and close both hands for five seconds.
  3. Do not touch anything, including fingertip to fingertip. Keep moving until verification finishes.
